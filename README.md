# Linc

**Your Android phone and your Windows PC, together.** Free.

Linc puts your phone on your PC and your PC in your hand: notifications, messages and calls on the PC,
one clipboard for both, files either way, your phone's screen in a window, and your PC's screen on the
phone.

## Get it

1. **On your PC:** download **[Linc for Windows](https://github.com/deloweProjects/LincApp/releases/latest)**
   (`Linc-Setup-….exe`) and run it.
2. **On your phone:** download **[Linc for Android](https://github.com/deloweProjects/LincApp/releases/latest)**
   (`Linc-….apk`) and open it.
3. Open Linc on the PC and choose **Add a phone**. Point your phone's camera at the code it shows. Done.

Or go to **[deloweprojects.github.io/LincApp](https://deloweprojects.github.io/LincApp/)** and tap the big buttons.

News, new versions and help: **[t.me/LincApp](https://t.me/LincApp)** on Telegram.

## What it does

- **Notifications** from your phone on the PC, with reply, dismiss and their own buttons.
- **Messages and calls**: read and send texts, see calls and dial from the PC.
- **Clipboard** on both: copy on one, paste on the other, even with Linc closed.
- **Files** either way: send, share, browse your phone's folders from the PC.
- **Mirror your phone** in a window on the PC, with sound, touch and keyboard, and buttons beside it.
- **Your PC on your phone**: see and control it, its sound, volume, brightness, media and lock.
- **Linc Bubble**: a small bubble on the desktop with what's going on (calls, music, files,
  messages) and quick shortcuts.
- **Connects by itself** over Wi-Fi, a USB cable, the PC's hotspot or the phone's, and moves between
  them without dropping. Bluetooth helps the two find each other.
- **Tools**: type on the phone from the PC, manage apps, take screenshots, check the phone's health,
  rescue a stuck phone, and hundreds of ready commands.
- **Desktop Mode**: Android's own desktop on your PC screen, where your phone supports it.
- **AI agents** (optional): let Claude or another AI agent use your phone through Linc, with you
  approving anything sensitive.

## Is it safe?

The link between your phone and PC is encrypted. Nothing goes through anyone's server: the two talk
to each other directly. Check what you download against `CHECKSUMS.txt` on each release.
