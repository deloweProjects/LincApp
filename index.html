L

Your phone and PC, together.

Notifications, messages, calls, clipboard, files and screens between your Android phone and Windows PC. Free.

Get Linc for WindowsDownload the setup and run it Get Linc for AndroidDownload the app and open it

On the download page, the Windows file is Linc-Setup-….exe and the phone file ends in .apk. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Three steps

  1. Install Linc on your PC.
  2. Install Linc on your phone.
  3. On the PC choose Add a phone and point your phone's camera at the code. That's it.

What it does

NotificationsYour phone's notifications on the PC, with reply.
Messages and callsText and see calls from the PC.
One clipboardCopy on one, paste on the other.
FilesSend and browse files either way.
Your phone on the PCIts screen in a window, with sound and touch.
Your PC on the phoneSee and control it from your hand.
Linc BubbleWhat's going on, one click away on the desktop.
Connects by itselfWi-Fi, cable or hotspot, and keeps going between them.
